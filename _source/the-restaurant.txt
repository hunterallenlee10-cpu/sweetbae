<h2> The Dining Room
Our main dining room has a stylish yet relaxed vibe, making it the perfect spot for a great meal with good company. The décor strikes a balance between sophistication and comfort, with warm lighting and thoughtfully placed accents that make the space feel welcoming. Local artwork celebrates the creative energy of the community. Whether guests are settling into cozy booths, grabbing a seat at the bar, or gathering around a larger table, there's a space for every kind of dining experience.

<h2> The Bar
Grab a drink while waiting for your reservation, or enjoy a meal with selected friends. Our expert bartenders will make your time at Sweetbay memorable!
Bar seats are available first come first serve.
<h2> The Wine Room
The perfect place for an intimate gathering of up to 14. The wine room provides a private space, complete with restroom and television for presentations. Reservations and a credit card deposit are required, and we have a minimum spend for your meal service. Call for details.
